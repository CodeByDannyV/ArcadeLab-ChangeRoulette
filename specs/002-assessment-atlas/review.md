## Assessment contract

A first independent exact impact prediction earns 60 points. A first independent correct risk band earns 40. A perfect streak counts consecutive 100-point first forecasts. Worked solutions and hints used before submission mark the ticket as assisted. The reference matrix is always available. Each scorecard denominator counts independent first forecasts. Replays and assisted tickets remain visible in the evidence log.

All tickets use a workshop matrix for simulated rollout risk. Full, partial and absent rehearsals represent increasing execution uncertainty. Recovery at or below the service tolerance, above tolerance through twice tolerance inclusive, and above twice tolerance represent increasing disruption. The matrix and replay explain how those inputs determine the band. Business scenarios, service placements and recovery estimates are fictional.

The assessment covers source-address reasoning and application of this published rubric. The exported JSON includes the observed first forecasts, score, rules, branch evidence and risk inputs for each assessed ticket.
